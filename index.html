E TO INTERACT

Welcome to E to Interact — an indie game studio dedicated to designing worlds, building games, and creating unforgettable interactive experiences.

Explore our world, discover our work, and enjoy your journey.

HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to the world

This whole site is one tall game world. You explore it as Vaugn, our ghost mascot — walking land to land instead of scrolling a page.

🌲 🏰 🏠 🛰 ☁

Five lands stack from the ground up: Forest, Castle, Studio, Space Station and Sky Temple.

Walk & run

WASDMove Vaugn (arrow keys work too)
WHead upward into the next land
ShiftHold to run
SpaceDash in the direction you're moving

The camera follows Vaugn automatically as you climb — no manual camera control needed.

Read the signs

EInteract with a glowing sign or door
🖱Or click signs, menu items & buttons directly

When you reach a signpost it lights up and a floating E prompt appears — press E to open that section (About, Games, Team, Posts, Contact).

Fast travel anywhere

Don't want to walk? Portal straight to any land with one key:

HHome / Spawn
GGames — the Castle
TTeam — the Studio
PPosts — the Space Station
CContact — the Sky Temple

Buttons & menu

EscOpen the menu — controls, settings, this guide
↻Top-left: reload the world anytime

The menu also has Settings for particles, text speed, reduced motion, and the day/night auto-cycle.

Turn the clock

Top-right there's a clock — sun on top, moon on bottom. It slowly turns on its own, or click it to instantly flip between day and night.

☀
☾
DAY

The active face lights up — the DAY or NIGHT badge glows below the clock. The whole world shifts: sky colour, stars, lanterns, fireflies, glowing screens and more.

Try it once you're in the world. Settings → Auto day/night cycle lets you stop the clock turning on its own.

HOW TO EXPLORE

This site is one tall world. You start at Spawn, at the very bottom.

Hold W to walk upward — through the Forest, into the Castle, past the Studio, out to the Space Station, and finally up to the Sky Temple.

Every land is joined by its own crossing: a forest trail, an old bridge, torchlit tower stairs, a launch elevator, and a sky bridge.

When you see a sign glowing, press E to read it.

The clock in the corner turns. Click it and the whole world changes.
